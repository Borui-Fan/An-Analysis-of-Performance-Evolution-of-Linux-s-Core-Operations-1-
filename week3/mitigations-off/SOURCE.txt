These three CSV files are original LEBench text from one Ubuntu 22.04.5 boot.
The kernel was 5.15.0-203-generic. GRUB was edited for that boot only, adding mitigations=off.
All three runs finished before the machine was rebooted. Do not open the CSV files in Excel.
